# Apple Design Guidelines

When working on the UI, adhere to the following Apple design principles:

1.  **Clarity**: Interfaces should be readable, crisp, and easy to understand. Use sufficient contrast and legible typography (e.g., San Francisco/Inter).
2.  **Deference**: The UI should help users interact with content without competing with it. Use subtle shadows, blurs (glassmorphism), and translucent backgrounds.
3.  **Depth**: Provide visual layers and realistic motion. Use smooth, spring-like animations and layering to indicate hierarchy.
4.  **Simplicity & Minimalism**: Focus on the primary task. Eliminate unnecessary visual clutter, borders, and heavy backgrounds.
5.  **Rounded Corners**: Use continuous (squircle) rounding for cards, buttons, and dialogs.
6.  **Subtle Gradients**: Use muted or carefully chosen gradients that don't overwhelm the eye.
7.  **Vibrancy and Translucency**: Employ background blurs to establish context and depth, similar to iOS/macOS materials.
